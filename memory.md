# SHRISTY HOME HEALTH CARE - Project Memory

> **Brand Slogan:** *"Care Today. Brighter Tomorrows."*  
> **Brand Identity:** Dedicated home care service delivering trusted, compassionate, and verified caregiving right at family doorsteps.

---

## 1. Visual & Brand Design (Light Theme)
- **Official Logo:** Emblem with house contour, leafy care embrace, silhouettes of Elder, Child, and Caregiver (`assets/logo.png`).
- **Brand Colors:**
  - **Forest Teal (Primary):** `#0b574f`
  - **Lush Leaf Green:** `#239452`
  - **Warm Ochre Gold:** `#b88a38`
  - **Compassion Coral:** `#ee6c6c`
  - **Pristine White & Soft Surfaces:** `#ffffff` & `#f8fbfa`
- **Typography:** Outfit (headings) & Plus Jakarta Sans (body).

---

## 2. Technology Stack Constraints (Strict)
- **HTML5:** Semantic, accessible standard markup.
- **CSS:** Custom CSS (`styles.css`) for responsive styling and brand theming.
- **Bootstrap 5:** Bootstrap 5.3.3 grid, components, and utilities (no Tailwind, etc.).
- **JavaScript:** Pure Vanilla JavaScript (`script.js`).
- **GSAP:** GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger for micro-interactions, scroll reveals, and counter animations.
- **No external heavy frameworks:** Strictly HTML, CSS, JS, Bootstrap, and GSAP only.
