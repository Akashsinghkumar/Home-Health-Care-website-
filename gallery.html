A Real Care Story

When Mr. Sharma came home after 3 weeks in hospital, his family didn't know where to begin

After a major surgery, Mr. Sharma's family was overwhelmed. Managing medications, wound care, and daily mobility felt impossible. They reached out to Shristy Home Health Care — and within 24 hours, a certified home nurse and caregiver were at their door.

Over the next six weeks, our team visited daily. The nurse monitored vitals, managed wound dressing, and coordinated with his doctor. The caregiver helped with meals, mobility, and simply being there. Mr. Sharma recovered fully — at home, with his family beside him.

Anita Sharma

Daughter — Post-Operative Care Client
