Privacy Policy

We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 2026

At Shristy Home Health Care, your privacy matters to us deeply. We understand that when you reach out to us — whether to ask about a service, book a care professional, or simply seek information — you are trusting us with personal details. This page explains clearly what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website or get in touch with us, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact details — your name, phone number, and email address when you submit our appointment or enquiry form.
  • Service details — the type of care you are enquiring about (for example, home nursing, elder care, physiotherapy).
  • Message content — any notes or questions you write in our contact form.
  • Device and usage information — basic data such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our website. This is collected automatically and does not identify you personally.

We do not ask for or store sensitive medical records or clinical documents through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you share with us is used only to serve you better. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and service requests promptly.
  • Arrange and confirm appointments for home healthcare services.
  • Provide you with relevant information about the care services you have requested.
  • Improve the quality and responsiveness of our website and services.
  • Send follow-up messages related to your care booking or enquiry, with your consent.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell your data to any third party.

3. Appointment & Contact Form Data

When you fill in our appointment or contact form, the details you provide — including your name, phone number, email, and selected service — are used exclusively to connect you with our care team. This information is handled with full confidentiality. Our staff access only the details needed to respond to your request. Once your care arrangement has been confirmed or your enquiry has been resolved, form data is not retained for unrelated purposes.

4. Cookies & Website Usage

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser — to improve your browsing experience. Cookies help us understand how visitors use our site so we can make it more useful and easier to navigate.

We use:

  • Functional cookies — to ensure the website works correctly on your device.
  • Analytics cookies — to understand page visits and user flow (no personal identity is captured).

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to access our website or contact us.

5. Data Protection

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data from unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure. Access to your information within our team is limited only to those who need it to respond to your care request.

While no website can guarantee absolute security over internet transmissions, we take every reasonable step to protect the data you share with us.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use a small number of trusted third-party tools to support its functionality, including:

  • Google Maps — used to display our service location. Google's own privacy policy applies to this embed.
  • Google Fonts — loaded from Google's CDN for consistent typography across the site.
  • YouTube — if embedded video content is shown, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

We do not share your contact details or form submissions with any of these services. These tools only interact with your browser to deliver their specific functionality.

7. Communication Policy

When you contact us via our website, we may follow up by phone or email to confirm your appointment or answer your enquiry. We will only contact you in relation to the request you made. We will not add you to mailing lists, promotional campaigns, or any automated messaging without your explicit consent.

If you receive any communication from us that you did not expect or wish to stop, please let us know and we will remove your details from our records immediately.

8. Your Rights

As someone who has shared information with us, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what information we hold about you.
  • Request that we correct any inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your personal data from our records.
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply reach out to us using the contact details at the bottom of this page. We will respond promptly.

9. Updates To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in how we operate or in response to feedback. When we make a meaningful change, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check back occasionally if you use our website regularly. Continued use of our website after any update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you would like to exercise any of your rights, please get in touch with us:
