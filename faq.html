Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our caregivers, verification, shift timings, and care protocols.

1. Caregiver Background & Verification

Every caregiver and nurse undergoes a 4-tier screening process: Aadhaar biometric identity check, official police criminal record clearance, prior employment and neighborhood reference cross-check in Patna, and an in-person behavioral temperament interview.

Yes, absolutely. Once our care manager assigns a suitable attendant for your family, their photo ID, name, and basic profile are shared with you on WhatsApp prior to their arrival.

2. Shifts, Timings & Plans

We provide four flexible scheduling options:
  • 12-Hour Day Shift: Typically 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM
  • 12-Hour Night Watch: Typically 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM
  • 24-Hour Live-in Care: Continuous residential care with day/night presence
  • Hourly Visits: Short visits for doctor accompaniment or specific procedures

Yes. We have trained female attendants and certified nurses available for female senior care, postnatal mother support, and infant Japa care upon your request.

3. Service Continuity & Safety

We maintain an active reserve pool of verified caregivers in Patna. If your regular attendant falls ill or has an emergency, our care coordinator assigns a trained replacement promptly so your loved one's routine is never broken.

Yes. Compatibility between the patient and caregiver is essential. If you feel the caregiver’s temperament or routine does not match your family's expectations, notify us and we will arrange a replacement at no extra charge.

4. Payments & Transparency
